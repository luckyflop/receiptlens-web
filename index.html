Zakat made simple

The Android app calculates Zakat with gold/silver nisab and a lunar-year hawl tracker.

Zakat calculator (Android)

Designed for the Gulf, in Arabic. Track your wealth and work out the 2.5% due with confidence.

Live nisab & hawl

Choose the gold (85g) or silver (595g) standard, fetch a live price per gram, and the app computes your nisab automatically. A hawl tracker follows the 354-day lunar year and tells you when Zakat becomes due.

Full asset breakdown

Add cash, gold, silver, trade goods, stocks and receivables, subtract debts, and get your zakatable total and the 2.5% amount — all with clear Arabic guidance.

This is a guidance tool, not a fatwa (أداة استرشادية وليست فتوى). For rulings specific to your situation, please consult a qualified scholar.
