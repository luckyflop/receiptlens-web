Privacy Policy

Receipt Lens AI · Last updated: June 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Receipt Lens AI (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Receipt Lens AI browser extension and its related services (the “Service”). By using the Service, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile information (such as your name). We use Google OAuth and request only the basic email and profile scopes.

Receipt data

When you scan a receipt, the image you choose is sent to our servers, where AI extracts details such as the merchant/vendor name, date, total amount, and tax. This extracted information may include financial details contained in your receipts, together with the currency conversion you request.

Locally stored data

Your preferences (for example, light/dark theme), your sign-in session, and your scanned receipt history are stored locally in your browser using the browser’s storage. This data stays on your device.

We do not collect your browsing history, and the extension does not read the content of the web pages you visit.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in.
  • To apply free-plan scan limits and to unlock Pro features for subscribers.
  • To process your receipt images and return the extracted, converted data you request.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on a small number of trusted providers, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In — used to authenticate you.
  • AI processing — receipt images are processed using AI (Google Gemini) on Google Cloud, solely to extract the data you request.
  • Polar.sh — processes Pro subscription payments. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details; payment is handled by the provider.

Receipt Lens AI’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google user data only to provide and improve the Service’s core features, and we do not transfer or sell this data except as needed to provide the Service, to comply with applicable law, or in connection with a merger or acquisition.

4. Data Storage & Retention

  • Local data (preferences, session, scan history) remains on your device until you sign out or clear it.
  • Account information (your email and your usage/subscription status) is retained on our servers while your account is active.
  • Receipt images sent for processing are used only to extract the requested data and are not retained longer than necessary to deliver the result.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties except the service providers listed above (acting on our behalf) or when required by law.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access or deletion: You can request access to, or deletion of, your account data by emailing us (see below).
  • Local data: Sign out to clear your session, and clear your scanned history from within the extension at any time.
  • Revoke Google access: You can remove the extension’s access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS). However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at luckyflopbtt@gmail.com.

Receipt Lens AI is a single-purpose expense tool. We collect only what is needed to scan your receipts, manage your plan, and let you export your expenses — nothing more.
